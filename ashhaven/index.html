AshHaven Roleplay Server

"Nearly twenty years ago, a band of miners split away from a nearby town because their pleas for better working conditions went unanswered. They founded AshHaven, and with the aid of technology and magic, transformed mining into a safer, more efficient trade. Prosperity followed, as they sold their excess resources to those in need. Yet now, the town has fallen silent…"

AshHaven is a Roleplay Server that prides itself on a very different approach to the roleplay server monotony. AshHaven is based almost entirely around Dungeons and Dragons, allowing for more in-depth character interaction, development, and growth!

Due to the nature of the server, players can continue roleplaying as a D&D character even while not actively in a session, so you aren't punished for not having time for a session!

We are a welcoming community that enjoys playing D&D and having fun. We are always looking for new members to [join our community](https://discord.gg/mmmEH7nAFN) and help us grow.
