AshHaven Roleplay Server

"Nearly twenty years ago, a band of miners split away from a nearby town because their pleas for better working conditions went unanswered. They founded AshHaven, and with the aid of technology and magic, transformed mining into a safer, more efficient trade. Prosperity followed, as they sold their excess resources to those in need. Yet now, the town has fallen silent…"



We run a hybrid roleplay server that blends tabletop sessions with persistent text-based roleplay — so the adventure never has to stop.

Every character starts with a one-shot introduction, giving you a natural way into the story and the town. From there, you can join scheduled campaign sessions whenever it suits you — and between those sessions, the world keeps moving. Form small parties, explore, gather resources, trade gear, or just enjoy the roleplaying moments that traditional campaigns rarely have room for: character relationships, rivalries, even PvP.

Since it's a fantasy setting full of wandering adventurers, stepping away is never a problem — your character's absence always makes perfect sense in the world. And if you ever want to change characters, that's just as easy to explain.



Join when you can. Play how you want. Your story fits here.

Ready to find out why the town fell silent? Join us.
